١ The institute المعهد

Connect. Aspire. Inspire.

More than a language. A wider world.

Established in 2009, Chennai Arabic Institute is a premier destination for all your Arabic needs, trusted by a client base spanning 10 varied industries.

Our focus is Modern Arabic with a commercial orientation, preparing learners for roles in the Arabic business sector. We teach through intensive and extensive courses, online Arabic classes, and a wide range of Arabic books for reading, writing and speaking.

Beyond the classroom, we translate documents, websites, passports, visas and certificates between Arabic and English, interpret for medical tourism, guide Arab visitors, and teach Arabic typing for self-employment and jobs in the Gulf.
